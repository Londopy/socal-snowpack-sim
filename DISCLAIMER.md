# Disclaimer

This project is a learning tool. It is **not an avalanche forecast** and must
not be used to decide whether a slope is safe.

- The weather is randomly generated. It is not real weather, past or present.
- The snowpack rules are simplified and hand-tuned. They have not been
  calibrated or validated against snow-pit data, avalanche observations or
  accident records.
- The day counts depend on thresholds chosen by the author. Change them and
  the numbers change.
- Elevation data has errors, and a 16 m grid smooths out the steepest terrain.
- Avalanches, falls on ice and rockfall kill people in these mountains. Take
  an avalanche course, carry and practise with rescue gear, read current
  observations, and make your own decisions in the field.

Southern California has no government avalanche forecast centre. The nonprofit
So Cal Snow Avalanche Center shares observations and runs courses. Send them
what you see.

Use of this code and its output is entirely at your own risk.
